A FIELD GUIDE FOR THE CURIOUS

How to get wonderfully lost

Click an island or choose one from the buttons under the map. Each island offers three actions, and your decisions change four qualities of the sky: Wonder, Care, Curiosity, and Mischief.

Collect six small relics by visiting all six islands. Combine any two relics in the Constellation Loom to reveal one of fifteen connections, then tap a discovered route to light it on the map. You can return whenever you like: the keepers react to your growing constellation. The Turn the sky button advances the atmospheric cycle, not a deadline. Meet four Weatherbound Visitors by trying Weather Studio presets and earn their postmarks. Once you welcome two visitors, visit the Living Constellations to make choices about their friendships. Then build three community landmarks in the Sky Commons by completing the required friendships.

Drag or use the arrow keys to pan the map. Scroll or use + and − to zoom. Press Escape to close a story. Your progress is saved on this device in your browser. Clearing browser data erases it; the postcard download preserves a summary.
